# Raiders Play Designer

In-house playbook editor for St. Regis Raiders (4th grade).

## Open it

Website (same book on every computer): https://chat-blip.github.io/raiders-play-designer/

Or double-click `index.html` / `Open Play Designer.bat` for a local copy. **Laptop pack** is still the offline game-day zip.

Edits on the website auto-save to GitHub. Coach PIN unlocks the editor.

## How to draw a play

1. Pick a play on the left, or **New play** from a formation (I / Split / QIP / ZIP).
2. **Drag** circles (offense) or triangles (defense) to move people.
3. Choose **Route**, **Block**, **Motion**, or **Ball path**, click a player, then click the field. Enter or right-click to finish.
4. Click a line, then drag the blue dots to bend it.
5. **Ball carrier** (red) and **Primary** (gold) match the Game 3 book.
6. **Flip L/R** mirrors the diagram. **Print page** is one landscape sheet. **Print all** prints every play in the list (one page each, in list order).

The Game 3 book (29 plays) is loaded as a starting point. Assignments are close, not traced from the PDF — move lines until they match how you want them taught.
